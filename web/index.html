Calibration

A spin-down: ride up to the speed shown, then stop pedalling and let the trainer coast to a stop. Do it after about 10 minutes of riding, when the trainer is warm, and again whenever the tyre pressure or the bike changes.

Di2 gears (experimental)

Shows your current gear, read from what the derailleur broadcasts (worked out from a GRX RX827; Shimano does not publish this). Shift once first to wake it, and close the E-TUBE app.
